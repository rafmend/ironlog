Privacy & Security

IronLog Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Executive Summary: IronLog is built with an offline-first, privacy-focused architecture. Your workouts, body metrics, and GPS routes reside solely on your device in a local SQLite database. We do not collect, cloud-host, sell, or share any personal data with third parties.

1. Data Controller

IronLog is an independently developed application focused on athletic strength tracking and user privacy. For any questions regarding your privacy, contact the developer email provided on the Google Play Store listing.

2. Data Handled by the Application

IronLog operates without user accounts or mandatory registration. It processes the following data:

  • Workout Data: Exercises, sets, repetitions, loads (kg/lbs), notes, and custom workout routines created by you.
  • Body Metrics: Body weight and body fat percentage voluntarily logged to track fitness progression.
  • Location & GPS Data: Only if you explicitly initiate an outdoor tracking session (running, cycling, walking), the app accesses your device's GPS sensor to calculate distance, pace, elevation, and route coordinates. This data is handled strictly on-device (in foreground or via a foreground service if your screen turns off during a run) and is stored exclusively in your local database.

3. Local Storage & Zero Third-Party Telemetry

All recorded logs are stored in a sandboxed local SQLite database on your phone. IronLog does not operate external analytics servers, user profiling trackers, or ad-targeting software.

You retain full data sovereignty: you can export all records into an unencrypted standard JSON backup, restore backups, or erase all data at any time from the in-app Settings menu.

4. Android Device Permissions

To provide its core tracking capabilities, IronLog may request the following standard Android permissions:

  • Precise & Approximate Location (GPS): Strictly required for real-time route tracing, speed, and distance calculations during outdoor endurance sessions. Never utilized for marketing or tracking outside active sessions.
  • Foreground Service: Keeps the outdoor GPS recorder and workout rest timer operating reliably if your screen locks during an active session.
  • Vibrate / Haptics: Provides tactile feedback when the rest timer reaches zero.
  • Wake Lock: Prevents screen timeout during active workout sessions when enabled in settings.
  • Post Notifications: Displays persistent status cards for active workouts or rest countdown alerts.

5. Third-Party Sharing

IronLog does not share, transmit, rent, or sell any user information, metrics, or location points to advertisers, third-party brokers, or cloud platforms.

6. Children's Privacy

IronLog is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the app does not transmit data over the Internet, no children's data is ever gathered or stored remotely.

7. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

In accordance with global privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA):

  • Right to Portability: You can export an instant JSON file containing all exercises, routines, workouts, and metrics at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: You can purge all application records immediately via Settings > Clear Data or by uninstalling the application from your device.

8. Policy Amendments

Any future revisions to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
